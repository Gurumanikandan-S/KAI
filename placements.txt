KPRIET PLACEMENTS AND CAREER DEVELOPMENT

The Career Development Centre (CDC) at KPR Institute of Engineering and Technology supports students in preparing for employment, internships and industry opportunities.

CAREER DEVELOPMENT CENTRE

The CDC works towards transforming students into industry-ready professionals.

Major objectives include:

* Supporting students in achieving placement opportunities
* Building student confidence
* Developing professional attitude
* Enhancing technical and employability skills
* Preparing students for industry requirements
* Providing career guidance and support

PLACEMENT TRAINING

KPRIET provides career and placement-oriented training activities such as:

* Career guidance programs
* Career support programs
* Communication skills training
* Personality development
* Aptitude preparation
* Logical reasoning preparation
* Verbal reasoning preparation
* Coding and programming skill development
* Resume preparation
* Interview preparation
* Industry-oriented skill development

CAREER READINESS

KPRIET conducts career-readiness activities to help students understand their strengths, identify skill gaps and prepare for suitable career pathways.

Training may include:

* Resume building
* Interview skills
* Aptitude training
* Communication development
* Professional skills
* Industry-relevant technical skills

INTERNSHIPS AND INDUSTRY CONNECT

KPRIET provides opportunities for students to connect with industries through:

* Internships
* Industry interactions
* Career fairs
* Workshops
* Expert sessions
* Industry projects
* Mentoring opportunities

The institution also provides a platform for organisations to collaborate with KPRIET students and graduates for internships, recruitment, workshops and career-related activities.

PLACEMENT OPPORTUNITIES

KPRIET supports students seeking opportunities across different sectors and career paths.

Placement opportunities may include:

* Core engineering companies
* Technology companies
* Product-based companies
* Service-based companies
* Other industry sectors

The availability of companies, recruitment drives, job roles and eligibility criteria may change from year to year.

CAREER ADVANCEMENT CELL

The Career Advancement Cell (CAC) supports students who are interested in higher studies, government examinations, PSU careers and other competitive examinations.

Career pathways supported by CAC include:

* GATE
* CEETA
* TNPSC examinations
* SSC examinations
* RRB examinations
* Engineering Service examinations
* UPSC
* Banking examinations
* TANCET
* CAT
* MAT
* IELTS
* TOEFL
* GRE
* Other higher-study and competitive examination pathways

KINDLE, STRIDE AND ASPIRE

KPRIET's strategic plan describes career development initiatives including:

* STRIDE for structured student skill development
* KINDLE for career readiness
* ASPIRE for GATE preparation

These initiatives are intended to support employability, higher studies and career preparation.

IMPORTANT NOTE

Placement statistics, salary packages, recruiters, job roles, eligibility criteria, placement schedules and recruitment drives can change over time.

KAI should not invent placement statistics or recruiter information.

For the latest placement information, students should check the official KPRIET Career Development Centre and placement announcements.
