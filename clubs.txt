KPRIET CLUBS AND STUDENT ACTIVITIES

KPR Institute of Engineering and Technology provides students with opportunities to learn beyond engineering through clubs, societies, technical activities, cultural activities and student-led programs.

KPRIET has a wide range of in-house clubs and societies. These activities help students develop technical skills, creativity, communication, teamwork, leadership and other professional skills.

SANGAMAM

Sangamam is a student activity group at KPRIET that brings together clubs focused on cultural, creative, social and personal development activities.

The KPRIET Clubs page currently lists the following clubs under Sangamam:

1. Art & Craft Club
   Conducts thought-provoking and educational activities through which students can explore culture, science and mathematics.

2. Dance Club
   Promotes different forms of dance among the KPRIET community and provides opportunities for students to showcase their talent.

3. Drama Club
   Provides opportunities for students interested in theatre and different aspects of drama.

4. Eco Club
   Encourages students to participate in meaningful environmental activities.

5. Film Club
   Provides opportunities for students to host shows and learn skills related to documentaries and video creation.

6. Music Club
   Provides opportunities for students to learn and develop a deeper understanding of music through various activities.

7. Photography Club
   Supports students interested in photography and media and helps them develop related skills.

8. Rotaract Club
   Organises community development activities and volunteering initiatives.

9. Tamil Mandram
   Promotes the value, culture and heritage of Tamil language and traditions.

10. Youth Parliament
    Helps students understand parliamentary processes and develops skills related to group discussions and social responsibilities.

11. Young Indians - YUVA
    Encourages students to participate in self-development, skill building, community service and nation building.

IGNITRRON

Ignitrron is a KPRIET student initiative focused on developing students' technical skills, leadership qualities and team coordination.

Its purpose is to provide a platform for students to develop and showcase their technical abilities in areas of interest.

The current KPRIET Ignitrron page lists the following clubs:

1. Centre for Drones and UAV Technology
   Promotes student involvement in aeronautical and aerospace-related areas and provides pathways into areas such as aviation and defence.

2. Business Club
   Provides a platform for students to practise leadership and entrepreneurial skills.

3. Electronics Club
   Provides opportunities for students interested in electronics and innovative electronics projects.

4. Internet Radio Club
   Promotes communication and speaking skills through creative interaction with a wider audience.

5. Science Club
   Promotes hands-on learning activities related to STEM.

6. Software Development Club
   Provides opportunities for students to collaborate, expand their knowledge and develop software using recent technologies.

7. Sweet Talkerz Club
   Helps students improve communication skills through leadership training and public-speaking practice.

8. Quiz Club
   Encourages students to improve their knowledge through quizzes and related activities.

9. Game Development Club
   Focuses on game development and encourages creativity, innovation and collaboration among aspiring developers.

10. Design Club
    Focuses on design and branding activities.

11. Astro Club
    Encourages interest in astronomy and space-related topics.

12. AR & VR Club
    Promotes learning and understanding of Augmented Reality and Virtual Reality technologies.

13. Finance Club
    Helps students develop knowledge and skills related to finance and financial decision-making.

CLUB PARTICIPATION

Students can join clubs based on their interests and participate in technical, cultural, creative, social and extracurricular activities.

Club participation can help students develop:

- Technical skills
- Communication skills
- Teamwork
- Leadership
- Creativity
- Problem-solving
- Professional networking
- Project experience

KPRIET CAMPUS LIFE

KPRIET describes campus life as including innovative, cultural, social, sporting and recreational activities.

The college website also states that KPRIET has more than 100 in-house clubs and societies managed by students and mentored by faculty.
