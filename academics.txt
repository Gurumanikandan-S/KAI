KPRIET ACADEMIC INFORMATION

KPRIET has a Centre for Academic Courses (CFAC).

The official academic section provides undergraduate and postgraduate regulations, curriculum and syllabus information.

Undergraduate regulations currently include:
- Regulations 2025
- Regulations 2021
- Regulations 2019

Postgraduate regulations include different regulation years for M.E./M.Tech and MBA programmes.

The official academic section also provides academic schedules for different academic years.

KPRIET follows a Choice Based Credit System (CBCS), which provides students with the freedom to choose courses from a list of departmental electives.
