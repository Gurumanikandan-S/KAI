KPRIET STUDENT SERVICES

KPR Institute of Engineering and Technology provides various student services to support academic development, career development, student welfare, professional development and overall student experience.

CENTRE FOR ACADEMIC COURSES

The Centre for Academic Courses (CFAC) supports academic-related activities and provides information related to academic regulations, curriculum, syllabi and academic schedules.

CAREER ADVANCEMENT CELL

The Career Advancement Cell supports students in developing skills and preparing for their academic and professional growth.

CAREER DEVELOPMENT CENTRE

The Career Development Centre (CDC) supports students in career development, training and placement-related activities.

CENTRE FOR FOREIGN LANGUAGES

The Centre for Foreign Languages provides opportunities for students to learn foreign languages and improve their language and communication skills.

CENTRE OF EXCELLENCE

KPRIET has Centres of Excellence that provide opportunities for students and faculty to develop technical knowledge, conduct projects and gain practical exposure in specialized technology areas.

RESEARCH AND DEVELOPMENT

The Research and Development activities at KPRIET encourage students and faculty to participate in research, innovation, projects and technology development.

ALUMNI RELATIONS

KPRIET maintains alumni-related activities to connect current students and alumni and encourage interaction, networking and knowledge sharing.

PRODUCT DEVELOPMENT

Product Development activities encourage students to transform ideas into practical products and solutions.

PROFESSIONAL SOCIETIES

KPRIET provides opportunities for students to participate in professional societies and student chapters related to different engineering and technology fields.

SANGAMAM

Sangamam provides opportunities for students to participate in cultural, creative, social and extracurricular activities through various student clubs.

SPORTS COUNCIL

The Sports Council supports sports and physical activities for students and provides opportunities to participate in sports-related events and activities.

WOMEN EMPOWERMENT CELL

The Women Empowerment Cell supports activities related to women's development, awareness, empowerment and a supportive campus environment.

MENTAL HEALTH AND HAPPINESS

KPRIET provides student support related to mental health and happiness as part of its student welfare initiatives.

ANTI-RAGGING CELL

KPRIET has an Anti-Ragging Cell to support a safe and respectful campus environment and address matters related to ragging.

STUDENT SUPPORT

Student services are intended to support students in areas including:

- Academic development
- Career development
- Technical development
- Research and innovation
- Communication skills
- Professional development
- Cultural activities
- Sports
- Student welfare
- Leadership and teamwork
