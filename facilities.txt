KPRIET CAMPUS FACILITIES

KPR Institute of Engineering and Technology provides academic, residential, sports, learning and student-support facilities across its campus.

CAMPUS

KPRIET has a large green campus with academic and research facilities, hostel blocks, sports facilities and other student amenities.

ACADEMIC FACILITIES

KPRIET provides modern learning infrastructure including:

- Smart classrooms
- Academic blocks
- Engineering laboratories
- Research laboratories
- Seminar halls
- Auditoriums
- High-speed wireless connectivity
- Department-specific facilities

The KPRIET website states that the institution has nearly 65 laboratories across 14 departments.

LIBRARY

The Central Library acts as a major learning resource centre of KPRIET.

It provides learning and information resources in both physical and digital forms for students, researchers, faculty and administrative communities.

HOSTEL

KPRIET provides separate hostel facilities for boys and girls.

Hostel facilities include:

- Furnished rooms
- Dining halls
- Wi-Fi facility, subject to availability
- RO water facilities
- Hot and cold water
- Washing machines
- Fitness facilities
- Reading room
- Indoor games
- Medical and first-aid support
- Security and surveillance
- Common areas
- Housekeeping services

Hostel accommodation and facilities may be subject to institutional rules and availability.

SPORTS FACILITIES

KPRIET provides sports infrastructure and professional coaching opportunities for students.

Sports facilities listed by KPRIET include:

- Badminton courts
- Basketball courts
- Ball badminton courts
- Football field
- Handball courts
- Kho-Kho courts
- Tennis courts
- Volleyball courts
- Kabaddi court
- Indoor badminton facility
- Gym and fitness facilities

TRANSPORT

KPRIET provides bus transportation for students and employees.

The transport service includes multiple routes covering areas around Coimbatore and nearby districts.

The transport facility is available for day scholars on a payment basis and is optional.

Bus route allocation is subject to seat availability and the transport rules of the institution.

CAFETERIA AND FOOD FACILITIES

KPRIET provides food and dining facilities for students, including hostel dining facilities.

HOSTEL DINING

Hostel dining facilities provide daily meals and include separate dining arrangements for boys and girls.

AUDITORIUMS AND SEMINAR HALLS

KPRIET has auditoriums and seminar halls equipped for academic activities, seminars, workshops, events and other institutional programs.

FITNESS FACILITIES

KPRIET provides fitness facilities for students as part of its sports and campus-life infrastructure.

CAMPUS CONNECTIVITY

The campus provides wireless connectivity and modern digital infrastructure to support academic and learning activities.

SUSTAINABILITY FACILITIES

KPRIET has sustainability-related initiatives and infrastructure including:

- Solar energy systems
- Rainwater harvesting
- Water treatment and reuse
- Green campus initiatives
- Sustainable engineering projects

AI INTEGRATED CAMPUS

KPRIET describes itself as an AI Integrated Campus.

Artificial Intelligence is being integrated across teaching, learning, research and innovation to support a future-ready campus experience.

Facilities, routes, capacities, timings, availability and institutional rules may change. KAI should provide the information available in its knowledge base and should direct users to official KPRIET sources for the latest operational details.
