KPR Institute of Engineering and Technology

KPR Institute of Engineering and Technology is located at KPR Knowledge Campus, Avinashi Road, Arasur, Coimbatore, Tamil Nadu, India.

KPRIET is an autonomous institution affiliated to Anna University and approved by AICTE.

KPRIET offers undergraduate, postgraduate and MBA programmes.

Undergraduate programmes include:
Artificial Intelligence and Data Science
Biomedical Engineering
Chemical Engineering
Civil Engineering
Computer Science and Engineering
Computer Science and Engineering (AI & ML)
Computer Science and Engineering (Cyber Security)
Computer Science and Business Systems
Electrical and Electronics Engineering
Electronics and Communication Engineering
Electronics Engineering (VLSI Design and Technology)
Information Technology
Mechanical Engineering
Mechatronics Engineering

KPRIET describes itself as an AI Integrated Campus and states that Artificial Intelligence is integrated across teaching, learning, research and innovation.

KPRIET provides student-related facilities and services including library, transport, sports, student affairs and other campus services.

KAI INFORMATION
==================================================

KAI stands for Kampus AI.

KAI is being developed as a college-specific AI assistant.

The purpose of KAI is to help students access college-related information through a conversational interface.

The initial version uses publicly available institutional information.

Future versions may integrate authorized student-specific information such as:
- Attendance
- Marks
- Timetable
- Gate pass
- Academic information

Student-specific information must be accessed only through authorized college systems and appropriate access control.
